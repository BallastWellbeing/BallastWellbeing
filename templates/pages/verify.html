Printed at the foot of the certificate. Format: BW-2026-0001-K4X7

What this checks

A Ballast Wellbeing certificate of completion records that a named participant attended a specific program and how many contact hours it ran to. It is not a professional designation, and it does not certify the holder in anything.

If a record needs correcting or removing, email hello@ballastwellbeing.com. Our privacy policy explains what this database holds and how to request removal.
