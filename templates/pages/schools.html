Who delivers your training Every course is delivered by a trained clinician or mental health professional.

Built around the school schedule

Courses are designed to fit within after-school staff meetings or PA days, so you can book training without arranging cover or losing instructional time.

Every participant leaves with a certificate of completion they can verify online.

How it works

  1. Get in touch. Tell us your school, roughly how many staff, and which course you're interested in.
  2. We confirm the details. Course, date, format, group size.
  3. Proposal. A written outline with a fixed price.
  4. Delivery. On site or virtual, with materials and a certificate of completion for every participant.

Common questions

How long are the courses?
Ninety minutes or half a day, depending on the course. Both fit an after-school staff meeting or a PA day.
How many staff can attend?
Up to 40 for the ninety-minute courses and up to 30 for the half-day, so most schools can train all staff in one session.
Can it be delivered virtually?
Yes, at a lower rate. On site works better for the half-day course because of the practice work.
What do participants get?
A workbook they use during the session, a one-page reference card, and a certificate of completion they can verify online.
Is this clinical training?
No. These are training courses for school staff, not clinical qualifications. They teach you to notice earlier, respond better, and hand over to the right person.
