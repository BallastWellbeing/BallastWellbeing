What independent schools are working without

Publicly funded boards are required to run a three-year mental health strategy, and the province funds a dedicated mental health lead in every district plus a provincial implementation team.

Independent schools carry the same student need with none of that infrastructure. The same rise in anxiety. The same staff burnout. The same parent expectations. Usually with one part-time guidance role and a head of school absorbing everything else.
