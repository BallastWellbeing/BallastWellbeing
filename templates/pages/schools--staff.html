Mental health is a leading driver of long-term disability leave across Ontario's education workforce. Independent schools feel this harder: smaller teams, more non-teaching duties, no federation supports, and a single departure that costs a term of stability.

Staff training is the cheapest retention instrument available to a school this size. It costs less than one replacement hire and it reaches everyone at once.
