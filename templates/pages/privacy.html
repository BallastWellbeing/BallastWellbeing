Who we are

Ballast Wellbeing delivers mental health training and education to schools and workplaces in Ontario. For any privacy question, contact {{ site.email }}.

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect, and why

Enquiry forms

When you submit an enquiry we collect your name, role, organization, email address, and optionally your phone number, along with the details you give us about your school or organization: type, size, sector, what you are interested in, your timeframe, and anything you add in the notes field.

We use it to reply to you, to prepare a proposal, and to keep a record of the enquiry. This is business contact information rather than personal information about your private life, and we treat it as such: it is not used for profiling and it is not enriched with data from anywhere else.

Email capture

If you subscribe to article updates we collect your email address and nothing else. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes the address rather than flagging it.

Certificate verification

The database behind certificate verification holds, for each certificate issued: the serial number, the participant's name, the program title, the contact hours, the completion date, and where applicable an expiry date.

It exists so that an employer or school can confirm a certificate is genuine. Anyone with a serial number can look up that record, which is the point of it, and the reason serial numbers include a random component rather than running in sequence. Lookups are rate limited and we do not store the IP addresses that make them.

If you are a participant and you want your record removed, email {{ site.email }} and we will delete it. You do not need to give a reason. Your certificate will then no longer verify, so tell us if you may need it for professional development records first.

Analytics

We do not use analytics. There is no page-view tracking, no cookies, and no third-party script of any kind on any page of this site.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or trade personal information
  • Run advertising pixels, retargeting, or third-party marketing trackers
  • Add you to a mailing list because you sent an enquiry. Those are separate consents
  • Route counselling information through any of the systems described on this page

Where it is stored, and who processes it

Enquiry and certificate data is held in a Supabase Postgres database hosted in Canada, in the Montreal (ca-central-1) region, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Give you a copy of it
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete it, subject to records we are legally required to keep
  • Withdraw consent for anything you previously agreed to

Email {{ site.email }}. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a privacy matter, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Breaches

If personal information in our control is lost or accessed without authorisation and there is a real risk of significant harm, we will notify the affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as PIPEDA requires, and we will tell you what happened and what we are doing about it.
