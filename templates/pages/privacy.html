Privacy policy

This policy applies to the training and education services of Ballast Wellbeing, including enquiry forms, the certificate database, and email correspondence.

{% if site.counselling_enabled %}

Looking for the counselling practice? Personal health information collected in the course of psychotherapy is governed separately and is addressed in our clinical privacy notice.

{% endif %}

Who we are

Ballast Wellbeing provides mental health training and education to schools and workplaces. Personal information is handled in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act. Privacy enquiries may be directed to {{ site.email }}.

Collection and use of personal information

Enquiry forms

When an enquiry is submitted, we collect the name, role, organization, and email address of the person enquiring, together with an optional telephone number and the details provided about the organization: type, size, sector, area of interest, timeframe, and any further notes.

This information is used to respond to the enquiry, prepare a proposal, and maintain a record of the request. It is business contact information. It is not used for profiling and it is not combined with data from other sources.

Email updates

Subscribers to article updates provide an email address and nothing further. Every message contains an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing deletes the address.

Certificate verification

The certificate database holds, for each certificate issued, the serial number, the participant's name, the program title, the contact hours, the completion date, and an expiry date where applicable.

Its purpose is to allow an employer or school to confirm that a certificate is genuine. Any person holding a serial number may look up the corresponding record. Serial numbers contain a random component so that they cannot be guessed in sequence. Lookups are rate limited, and the IP addresses that make them are not stored.

Participants may request removal of their record by writing to {{ site.email }}. Once a record is removed, the certificate will no longer verify.

Website analytics and cookies

This site uses no analytics. There is no page-view tracking, no cookies, and no third-party script on any page{% if site.counselling_enabled %}, including the counselling pages{% endif %}. Fonts are served from our own server.

{% if site.counselling_enabled %}

Booking is not carried out on this site. The booking links on the counselling pages lead to Jane, a separate scheduling service on its own website. Once that link is followed, Jane's privacy policy applies to what happens there, and nothing on this site records that the link was followed.

{% endif %}

Limits on use and disclosure

Ballast Wellbeing does not:

  • sell, rent, or trade personal information
  • operate advertising pixels, retargeting, or third-party marketing trackers
  • add enquiries to a mailing list, which requires separate consent
  • process counselling information through any of the systems described in this policy

Storage and third-party processors

Enquiry and certificate data is held in a Canadian-hosted database located in Montreal, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Access and correction

On request, Ballast Wellbeing will:

  • confirm what personal information it holds about you
  • provide a copy of that information
  • correct inaccurate information
  • delete information, subject to records that must be retained
  • withdraw consent previously given

Requests may be sent to {{ site.email }} and will be answered within 30 days. There is no charge.

Questions and complaints

Questions about this policy may be directed to {{ site.email }}. Complaints about the handling of a privacy matter may be directed to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Breach notification

Where personal information under our control is lost or accessed without authorization and a real risk of significant harm exists, Ballast Wellbeing will notify the affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and will set out what occurred and the steps taken in response.
