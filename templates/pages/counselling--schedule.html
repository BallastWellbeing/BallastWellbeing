50 minutes or 30?

50 minutes is standard, and what most sessions should be — particularly early on, and any time we're working with trauma or something difficult.

30 minutes works well for check-ins between longer sessions, following up on something specific, or maintenance once things are steadier. It isn't a cheaper version of a full session — it's a different thing, and it's not suitable for trauma work or a first appointment.

If you're not sure, book 50. We can always finish early.
