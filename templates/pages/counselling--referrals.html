Information for referring professionals

Ballast Wellbeing accepts referrals from physicians, legal and insurance professionals, employers, and employee assistance providers. All sessions are virtual, by secure video or telephone.

Areas of clinical focus

  • Trauma and post-traumatic stress. Developmental trauma, single-incident trauma, and critical incident exposure.
  • Motor vehicle accident trauma. Driving and passenger anxiety, collision-related avoidance, and somatic distress following a collision.
  • Panic and anxiety disorders. Panic attacks, agoraphobic avoidance, and generalized anxiety.
  • Occupational stress and burnout. Chronic workplace stress, burnout, and workplace-related trauma.
  • Grief, adjustment, and relational difficulties. Complex grief, major life transitions, and relationship distress.

Making a referral

Referrals and enquiries may be sent to {{ site.email }}. We will confirm receipt and arrange an initial appointment directly with the client.
