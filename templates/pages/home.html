Why Ballast

Ballast is the weight carried low in a ship's hull. It isn't cargo. It does nothing useful on its own. Its only job is to keep the vessel upright when the water turns.

That's the work. We don't hand people more to carry. We build what sits underneath — so a teacher facing a dysregulated classroom, a manager hearing a hard disclosure, or a student in the middle of exam season stays steady under load.

What we teach

Schools

Independent schools sit outside the publicly funded mental health support system. No board mental health lead, no ministry-funded programming, no implementation coach. We build that layer for you — student sessions, staff PD, and parent evenings, priced for schools that don't have a district budget behind them.

See school programs →

Workplaces

Mental health is now the leading driver of disability claims in Canada, and Ontario employers carry a legal duty of care for psychological safety. We train the people who determine whether that duty is met: the managers having the conversation.
