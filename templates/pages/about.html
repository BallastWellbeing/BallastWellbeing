Norman Rehman, M.A.
Member, Ontario Association of Mental Health Professionals

I spent two years teaching in Ontario schools before moving into counselling psychology. That order matters. I know what it's like to be handed a class of thirty and told that a child's home situation is now partly your problem, with no training for it and nowhere to put it.

Ballast exists because the training available to schools and employers usually comes from one of two places: consultants who have never done the work, or national vendors delivering the same slide deck in every province. Neither is much use at four o'clock on a Tuesday when a student is escalating or an employee has just told you something you weren't ready for. Everything here is built to be used that afternoon.
