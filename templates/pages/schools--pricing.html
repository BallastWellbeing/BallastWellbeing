Independent schools rarely have a current wellbeing policy, and many are running on a document adapted from a public board a decade ago. We review what you have, identify the gaps, and give you a written set of recommendations. Includes a two-hour working session with your leadership team.

Multi-school pricing

Schools that contract together — faith-based networks, Montessori associations, small independent groups — pay less.

  • 2 schools: 10% off
  • 3–4 schools: 15% off
  • 5+ schools: 20% off plus one shared full-day PD included

A shared regional PD day typically works out to $700–800 per school.
