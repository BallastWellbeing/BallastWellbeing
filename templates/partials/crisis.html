If you need help right now

This practice doesn't provide emergency or crisis services, and messages aren't monitored outside business hours.

If you're in immediate danger, call 911.

For urgent mental health support anywhere in Canada, call or text 988 — the Suicide Crisis Helpline, available 24 hours a day.

In Toronto, the Gerstein Crisis Centre operates 24 hours at 416-929-5200. The Toronto Community Crisis Service, a non-police mobile crisis response, is reached by calling 211.
