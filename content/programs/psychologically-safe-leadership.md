covers: >-
  CSA Z1003 sets the benchmark against which employer conduct on psychological
  health and safety is measured, and it is organised around thirteen workplace
  factors rather than around individual wellbeing. Leaders work through which of
  those factors their own decisions actually control, since most sit with
  management rather than with an employee assistance programme. The session is
  built around documented practice, because documented training of the people who
  apply a policy is what demonstrates reasonable care.
outcomes:
  - "Map the thirteen psychosocial factors in CSA Z1003 against decisions they personally make"
  - "Identify the two or three factors where their own team carries the most exposure"
  - "Redesign one recurring management practice to reduce psychological risk, and write it down"
  - "State what the Occupational Health and Safety Act and WSIB actually require, without overstating it"
  - "Distinguish accommodation obligations from performance management, and handle both without conflating them"
  - "Brief their own managers on what changed and why, using language that survives repetition"
